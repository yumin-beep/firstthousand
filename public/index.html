    How it works

    1. Core research. The agent resolves your comparable games in Qloo, pulls their taste tags, asks for cross-domain recommendations (music, films, podcasts, brands, TV), similar games, and where the audience is concentrated.
    2. Follow-up. A model reads that evidence and may make a few more Qloo calls where results were empty or vague.
    3. Writing. The model writes the brief as structured claims. Each claim lists the names it mentions and the evidence ids it relies on.
    4. Checking. Code, not the model, checks every named item against the cited Qloo results. Claims are marked grounded partial not in evidence or interpretation.
    5. Comparison. The same model writes a brief without Qloo. Its names are looked up in Qloo to show which ones exist at all.

    Qloo results describe aggregate taste affinities. They are not predictions about any person, and not sales forecasts.
